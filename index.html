Key Projects

🥁

Beat Busters (BB Groups)

EYFS / KS1 Group Intervention Motor Coordination

A music and games-based group intervention designed to boost engagement and reduce stigma through positive group dynamics. Structured to move from energy to calm, every session ends with a surprise.

Session arc: Warm-up → Active Session → Calm-down → Surprise Activity

Focus areas: Motor coordination, social communication, turn-taking, emotional co-regulation

🧠

Sensory Motor Groups

Sensory Integration Planning & Delivery

Structured sensory motor group sessions designed to address proprioceptive and vestibular needs in young learners, with session plans adapted to individual sensory profiles.

Approach: Evidence-based sensory diet activities embedded in group play contexts to normalise support and build peer connection.

✏️

Handwriting Screener Programme

Fine Motor Data-Driven

A classroom-level screening process using the Handwriting Without Tears framework to identify students requiring fine motor support, with follow-up intervention plans.

Outcome: Targeted referral pathways and classroom strategy recommendations for teachers.
